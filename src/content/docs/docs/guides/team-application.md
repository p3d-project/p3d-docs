---
title: Team Application
---

In [Discord](https://discord.gg/CQnkc5gS6a), new applicants should go to *#roles* & grab the @Applicant role, then check out #apply-to-the-team & follow the instructions there. You will be pinged in the #approvals or #rejections regarding your application status
- If approved, you will be removed as an @Applicant & will be granted team roles!
- If rejected, you will be removed as an @Applicant & can apply again later
